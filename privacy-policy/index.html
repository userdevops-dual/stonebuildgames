Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information StoneBuild Games collects in our games and on our website, how we use it and the choices you have.

Last updated: November 4, 2026

1. Who we are

StoneBuild Games ("StoneBuild", "we", "us") is a game development studio. We develop and publish games for Android, iOS and PC (the "Games") and operate the website stonebuildgames.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy applies to all Games published by StoneBuild Games on Google Play, the Apple App Store, Steam or other stores, and to the Site.

If you have any questions, contact us at support@stonebuildgames.com.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

When you contact us by email or through the Site, we receive your name, email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to respond to you.

2.2 Information collected automatically in our Games

Our Games may collect, directly or through the third-party services listed in Section 4, the following categories of information:

  • Device information: device model, operating system version, language, screen size, memory, and general performance data.
  • Identifiers: the advertising identifier of your device (Google Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where you have allowed it), app instance identifiers and similar pseudonymous identifiers.
  • Gameplay data: progress, levels completed, scores, in-game choices, session length and feature usage.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and error logs that help us fix bugs.
  • Approximate location: country or region derived from your IP address, used for localisation, analytics and complying with regional law. We do not collect precise GPS location unless a Game asks for it and you agree.
  • Purchase information: if you make an in-app purchase, the store (Google Play or Apple) processes the payment. We receive a confirmation of the purchase and the item bought, never your card or bank details.

2.3 Information collected on the Site

The Site is a static website. Fonts, scripts and images are served from our own domain, and the Site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested, time of request) for security and reliability. The Stone Stack mini-game stores your best score only in your own browser (local storage); it is never sent to us. If we add analytics in the future, this policy will be updated first.

3. How we use information

  • To run the Games and the Site and keep them working on your device.
  • To save your progress and restore purchases.
  • To understand how players use the Games so we can improve levels, balance and features.
  • To find and fix crashes and bugs.
  • To show advertising in Games that are free to play, and to measure that advertising.
  • To prevent fraud, cheating and abuse.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations and store policies.

4. Third-party services

Our Games are built with industry-standard tools and services. Depending on the Game, these may include:

  • Google Play Services and Google Play Games Services (sign-in, achievements, leaderboards, cloud saves) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob (advertising) — How Google uses data in ads
  • Firebase (analytics, crash reporting, remote configuration) — Firebase Privacy
  • Unity (game engine, Unity Ads, Unity Analytics) — Unity Privacy Policy
  • Apple Game Center and App Store (sign-in, purchases) — Apple Privacy Policy

Each Game's store listing (for example the Google Play "Data safety" section) lists exactly which of these services that Game uses. These providers process data under their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Advertising

Some Games are free to play and supported by ads. Ad networks may use your advertising identifier to show ads that are more relevant to you and to limit how often you see the same ad. You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalisation".
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track".

Where required by law, a Game will ask for your consent before showing personalised ads.

6. Children's privacy

Our Games are intended for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a Game is designed for children, it is published under the relevant store family programme and uses only services approved for children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, email support@stonebuildgames.com and we will delete it.

7. Data retention

We keep gameplay and diagnostic data only as long as needed for the purposes above, typically no longer than 24 months, after which it is deleted or aggregated. Support emails are kept for up to 24 months. Purchase records are kept as long as required by tax and accounting law.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Union or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these rights, email support@stonebuildgames.com. We respond within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Data and account deletion requests

To request deletion of your data from any StoneBuild game, email support@stonebuildgames.com with the subject "Data deletion request". Include the name of the game and, if the game shows one, your player ID. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Deleting your data removes your progress, scores and any linked account data from our systems. Some information, such as purchase receipts, may be retained where the law requires it. You can also uninstall a Game at any time to stop all further collection by that Game.

11. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our Games and the law change. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. For significant changes, we will notify you inside the affected Game or on this page.
