How we work

How we build a game, from idea to launch

Every StoneBuild Games project follows five stages: discovery (about one week), a playable prototype (two to three weeks), production in two-week sprints, polish and QA, then launch and LiveOps. You get a playable build and a written changelog at the end of every sprint.

  1. 1

    Discovery

    We define the core loop, target players, platforms and scope, then agree on a plan and a budget.

    About 1 week
  2. 2

    Prototype

    A playable vertical slice that proves the fun before we commit to full production.

    2 to 3 weeks
  3. 3

    Production

    Two-week sprints. Art, code and design land together, with a new build in your hands every sprint.

    Sprint based
  4. 4

    Polish & QA

    Device testing, performance passes, balancing and store policy checks until the game is release-ready.

    Final sprints
  5. 5

    Launch & LiveOps

    Store listings, soft launch, analytics and the updates that keep players coming back.

    Ongoing
Questions

Frequently asked questions

What does a game development studio do?

A game development studio designs, builds, tests and ships games. StoneBuild Games covers the whole cycle: game design, programming in Unity, Unreal Engine or Godot, art and animation, multiplayer and backend, QA on real devices, and publishing on Google Play, the App Store and Steam.

How long does it take to build a mobile game?

A playable prototype takes two to three weeks after a one-week discovery. A small casual game can ship in two to three months of two-week sprints; a 3D or multiplayer game usually takes longer. We give a dated plan after discovery.

How much does it cost to develop a game?

It depends on scope. Our inquiry form uses four ranges: under $5k for a prototype or a small feature, $5k to $15k for a casual mobile game, $15k to $50k for a mid-size 3D or multiplayer title, and $50k+ for larger productions. Every quote follows a written scope.

Who owns the source code and art?

You do. Source code, art files, design documents and store accounts stay in your name, and the contract says so. There is no lock-in.

Which engines and platforms do you work with?

Unity, Unreal Engine and Godot, in 2D and 3D, for Android, iOS, PC (Steam) and the web. Backend work uses Firebase, Photon and Google Play Games Services.

Where is StoneBuild Games based?

StoneBuild Games is a remote studio and works with clients worldwide over email and video calls. Reach us at support@stonebuildgames.com; we reply within two business days.

Have a game in mind?

Tell us about it. We reply within two business days with questions, a rough scope and next steps.
