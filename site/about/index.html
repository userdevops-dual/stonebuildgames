About the studio

A studio built on solid foundations

StoneBuild Games is an independent, remote game development studio that builds 3D, 2D and multiplayer games for Android, iOS and PC with Unity, Unreal Engine and Godot. We build our own titles and develop games for clients, with the same team, the same pipeline and the same standards.

Why we exist

Most games do not fail on the big idea. They fail on details: a frame drop on a mid-range phone, a save that does not restore, a store review that gets rejected the week of launch. StoneBuild exists to get those details right, so the idea gets a fair chance.

The name says what we do. Stone, because we build things meant to last: clean code, documented systems and assets you can keep working with for years. Build, because we ship. Every sprint ends with a playable build, and every project ends on a storefront.

We work with Unity, Unreal Engine and Godot, in 2D and 3D, for Android, iOS, PC and the web. We are deliberately small, so the people who scope your game are the people who build it. The 3D scene on our home page and the Stone Stack mini-game are our own real-time work.

  • Performance first

    We target a stable 60 fps on mid-range Android phones, small download sizes and fast first loads, and we measure them on real devices every sprint.

  • A build every sprint

    Two-week sprints with a playable build and a written changelog each time. You always know where the game stands.

  • Store compliance built in

    Privacy policies, data safety forms, age ratings and content rules are handled during production, so launch day is not a surprise.

  • You own everything

    Source code, art files and store accounts stay in your name. Clear contracts, clear IP, no lock-in.

How we work

From idea to launch, in the open

Discovery, prototype, production, polish, launch. You see a playable build every sprint and know exactly what ships next.
